Financial Crime Analyst

Ref: FCA8B1 | Salary: $110,000-$150,000

In this position you will work with the cyber security team to identify and analyze potential financial crimes while ensuring compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorism financing (CTF) laws

Key Responsibilities

  1. Monitor transaction queues for unusual or high-risk patterns.
  2. Conduct Know Your Customer (KYC) and Know Your Business (KYB) checks.
  3. Analyze and investigate potential financial crimes.
  4. Investigate internal fraud, scams, and cyber threats.

Essential Requirements

  • Strong analytical and data interpretation abilities.
  • Experience with financial crime detection tools and techniques.
  • Problem-solving and clear communication skills.
  • Relevant study in criminology, accounting, or risk management.

Preferable Requirements

  • 1-3 years working in risk management, banking operations, audit, or regulatory compliance.
  • Experience with financial crime detection software and tools.
  • Knowledge of financial regulations and compliance standards.
